Pesticide label signal words: Danger, Poison, Warning, and Caution indicate increasing or moderate hazard warnings depending on the label system. Always follow the product label.

General pesticide decontamination: move away from exposure, remove contaminated clothing and PPE, keep contaminated clothing separate from household laundry, wash skin and hair with soap and running water, rinse exposed eyes with clean water for at least 15 minutes, and carry the product label to medical care.

Organophosphate and carbamate pesticides can affect the nervous system. Warning symptoms include headache, dizziness, vomiting, sweating, salivation, pinpoint pupils, muscle twitching, breathing difficulty, confusion, seizures, and unconsciousness. Swallowing or severe symptoms require emergency medical care.

Environmental safety: avoid pesticide drift, runoff, and contamination of ponds, wells, rivers, livestock areas, food storage, and pollinator habitats. Do not pour leftover pesticide into drains or water bodies.
